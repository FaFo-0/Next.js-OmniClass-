# Editorial redesign: tool research and direction

The first version repeated a header/body/screenshots pattern. This version uses seven distinct compositions: a conversation-led cover, an one-to-one lesson conversation, vocabulary-card collage, yellow feedback poster, reading spread, vocabulary collection and trial-price poster. Flowing lines connect ideas; Arial bold contrasts with Georgia italic, both available on this Mac. Purple/yellow/cream retain the established brand. Original vector logo and actual screenshots are unchanged; screenshots are cropped and rotated as editorial inserts.

No DALL·E, ImageGen or other image-generation service was used. The editable artwork is authored programmatically with SVG; Pillow measures type and sharp renders PNG exports. Screenshots are real raster inputs and are not AI reconstructed. Dialogue on the cover and slide 2 is an illustrative English learning example, not an attributed quotation or a captured lesson.

Current tools reviewed via official sources:

- [Recraft editable vector generation](https://www.recraft.ai/ai-vector-generator): relevant for optional future illustration assets. Not invoked for this job.
- [Figma Buzz](https://www.figma.com/buzz/): brand templates and editable social assets. Not used for this job.
- [Affinity/Canva editable exchange](https://www.canva.com/help/sharing-export-to-canva/): SVG is an editable vector exchange format.

The priority here was a new art direction and controllable, editable layout. No account connection, paid generation or third-party upload was required. The designs are original rather than copies of searched references.
