Created 28 October 2026. Display name + bio options for `@omnica.english`, derived from the marketing context at `~/Omni-English/.agents/product-marketing.md`, `POLICY.md`, and the already-published introduction carousel.

## What is wrong with the current profile

**Current display name:** `Omnica — Умный Английский 🗣️`
**Current bio:** `All in one English learning platform`

| # | Problem | Why it costs something |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The name field carries no searchable phrase | Instagram indexes the *name* field for search. `Умный Английский` is not what anyone types. `английский для взрослых` / `английский онлайн` are. This field is free reach that is currently spent on nothing. |
| 2 | `Умный Английский` is a claim, not a description | Dozens of CIS schools use "умный / лёгкий / простой английский". It reads generic and promises smartness instead of naming the customer or the situation. |
| 3 | The bio is in English | The documented audience is Russian-speaking adults in Kazakhstan, and the working language of the product is Russian. The most-read line on the profile is written in the language the customer reads second. |
| 4 | `All in one` is the most saturated phrase in EdTech | It describes the software ("platform"), not what the learner gets. It also cannot be substantiated as a claim the way a feature can. |
| 5 | No audience, no price, no next step | The bio says nothing about who it is for, what the entry point costs, or what to do next. The link list is the only path and it has no invitation attached. |
| 6 | Name shortens the brand | The lockup everywhere else — logo, domain, handles, pins — is **Omnica English**. The name field shows only `Omnica`, so search for the full brand does not match it. |
| 7 | The published caption has the wrong currency | The carousel slide reads `1 500 ₸` (correct, per `POLICY.md` line 30). The caption on the live post reads `1 500 ₽`. Rubles on a Kazakhstan offer is a trust break. |

## Comparison

| | A: category first | B: differentiator first | C: free library first |
|---|---|---|---|
| Search terms in name | `английский для взрослых` — high volume, exact intent | `английский, который не забывается` — no search volume | `бесплатная библиотека` — mid volume, high curiosity |
| Who it is for | stated in line 1 | stated in line 1 | implied by "с преподавателем", weaker |
| Differentiator | shown as a chain (lesson → cards → review) | stated as a promise | shown as free access |
| Entry point named | 1 500 ₸ trial | "начать бесплатно" | 1 500 ₸ trial |
| Cold-traffic strength | medium | low — promise without proof | high — "free" is the strongest cold hook |
| Warm-traffic strength | high — states the offer and the price | medium | high |
| Risk | none; slightly generic | subjective claim, unverifiable | free-first may attract non-buyers |

## Recommendation

**Use Option A**, with one change: keep the 4th line as the trial, and change what the profile's clickable link points to.

Reasoning:
- Option A is the only one that fixes the *structural* problem — the name field carrying a phrase people actually search. B's name is a slogan: nice on a billboard, invisible in search. C is the strongest hook but "бесплатная библиотека" pulls free-seekers who are the furthest from a 1,500 ₸ booking; it works better as post #2 (which the published caption already is) than as the profile's identity.
- A states audience ("для взрослых"), mechanism ("слова с урока → карточки"), the free layer, and the paid entry point with its real price, in four short lines — a stranger can answer *who for, what I get, why now, what next* in under 15 seconds, which is the completion criterion in the marketing workflow.
- It stays consistent with the published material: the carousel and the pinned post already lead with "Начни бесплатно" as the secondary entrance, so the profile promising the same free layer is reinforcement, not a new claim.

**Open decision — the profile's single CTA.** The business goal in the context is qualified trial bookings, but a cold Instagram visitor rarely pays 1,500 ₸ before trusting the account. Two consistent choices:

| Choice | Link 1 | Link 2 | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Recommended** — free-first | `omnicaenglish.com` (sign-up / library) | `wa.me/77066993942` — "Вопросы? Напишите нам" | Captures the lead on the owned platform; the trial is offered inside onboarding and in every post |
| Trial-first | trial page on `omnicaenglish.com` | WhatsApp | Direct to the 1,500 ₸ booking; fewer leads, higher intent |

Pick one and state it — the profile must not split traffic between two competing conversions.
