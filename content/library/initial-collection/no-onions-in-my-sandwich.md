# No Onions in My Sandwich

Ben is at a small café. He has ten minutes before his English class. He looks at the menu above the counter.

Ben: Hello. Can I have a cheese sandwich, please?

Server: Of course. With tomato and onion?

Ben: With tomato, please, but no onions.

Server: No onions. Anything to drink?

Ben: A small coffee with milk, please.

Server: Is that to eat here or take away?

Ben: To take away. My class starts soon.

Server: That's five pounds fifty.

Ben pays and waits by the window. The server puts a bag on the counter.

Server: Cheese sandwich and a small coffee.

Ben: Thank you. Sorry, is this my sandwich? There are onions in it.

Server: Oh, I'm sorry. That's another customer's order. Your sandwich is here. Tomato, no onions.

Ben: That's right. Thank you.

Server: Here's your coffee. Be careful. It's hot.

Outside, Ben checks his bag. He has his sandwich, a napkin and a spoon. His classroom is just across the street.
