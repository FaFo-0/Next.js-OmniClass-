# A Quiet Place for Tea

Noor lives in a small flat. Her neighbour Eva lives upstairs. They say hello on the stairs, but they do not often talk.

On Saturday, Eva sees Noor with a bag of oranges.

"Those look nice," she says.

"They are for my sister. She is here today. Do you want to come for tea?"

"Thank you. What time?"

"Is four o'clock good?"

"I work until four. Is five OK?"

"Yes. Come at five."

At five, Eva knocks on Noor's door. She has some biscuits.

"Come in," says Noor. "This is my sister, Amal."

The kitchen is warm. A small child is asleep in the next room, so they speak quietly. Eva puts the biscuits on a plate. Amal makes tea.

They talk about the street and the shops near their homes. Eva knows a good place for fresh bread.

"Let's go there tomorrow," says Noor.

"In the morning?"

"Yes. After breakfast."

Eva smiles. Now she has someone to go with.
