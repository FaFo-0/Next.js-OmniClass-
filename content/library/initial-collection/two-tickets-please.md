# Two Tickets, Please

Maya and her brother Sami are at a bus station. They want to visit Green Park. Maya speaks to the woman at the ticket desk.

Maya: Hello. Two tickets to Green Park, please.

Woman: Single tickets or return tickets?

Maya: Sorry. What is a return ticket?

Woman: You can go to the park and come back with it.

Maya: Return tickets, please. How much are they?

Woman: Six pounds for two people.

Maya: Can I pay by card?

Woman: Yes. Put your card here.

Sami: Is the bus outside?

Woman: Yes, but that's the bus to the airport. Your bus is number eight. It stops across the road.

Maya: Number eight. Thank you. What time is the next bus?

Woman: At ten fifteen. In five minutes.

Sami: Does it stop near the park entrance?

Woman: Yes. The stop is next to a flower shop.

Maya: Great. Thank you for your help.

They cross the road at the lights. Sami checks the number before they get on.
