# Can We Move the Meeting?

Daniel works in a small design office. On Tuesday morning, he calls his colleague Aisha. They have a meeting with a client at two o'clock.

Daniel: Hi, Aisha. Have you got a minute?

Aisha: Yes. What's the matter?

Daniel: The client just called. Her train is late. She can't get here before three.

Aisha: I have another meeting at three thirty. Could we meet online tomorrow morning instead?

Daniel: She wants to see the paper samples, so she needs to come to the office.

Aisha: Right. How about Thursday at ten?

Daniel: I'm visiting our supplier then. I'm free after lunch, though.

Aisha: Thursday at one thirty works for me. We can use the small meeting room.

Daniel: Shall I call her and ask?

Aisha: Yes, please. And don't cancel today's meeting in the calendar until she agrees.

Twenty minutes later, Daniel calls again.

Daniel: Thursday is fine. She asked how long the meeting will take.

Aisha: About forty-five minutes. We need to choose the paper and check the final colours.

Daniel: I'll send everyone a new invitation with the room number.

Aisha: Thanks. Can you put the sample list in it too? Then she can prepare her questions.

Daniel sends the invitation and removes the old one. Aisha accepts it before she starts her next task.
