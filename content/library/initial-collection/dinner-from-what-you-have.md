# Dinner from What You Have

At six o'clock, you are hungry. You open the fridge and think, "There's nothing for dinner." But is the fridge really empty?

Before you order food, put three things on the kitchen table: something that needs cooking soon, something that will make the meal filling, and something that adds flavour.

Last Wednesday, Karim found half a cabbage and two carrots. He also had rice in the cupboard. His sister wanted to buy chicken, but the shops were busy and they were both tired.

They cut the vegetables into small pieces. Karim cooked the rice while his sister fried an onion. She added the carrots first because they were harder than the cabbage. Then she mixed everything together with a little salt and lemon juice.

The meal wasn't a special recipe. It was simply a good use of the food they already had. They saved one portion for lunch the next day.

This habit doesn't mean you must eat food you don't like or keep food that is no longer safe. Check it carefully. It means looking before shopping.

Keep a short list on your fridge: "Use these first." Write down the vegetables, opened packets and leftovers that need attention. When you go shopping, take a photo of the list.

You may still need a few ingredients. But you will have a plan, and fewer forgotten carrots at the back of the fridge.
