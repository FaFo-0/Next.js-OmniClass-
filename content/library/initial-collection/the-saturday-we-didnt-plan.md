# The Saturday We Didn't Plan

Olga and Leila planned to spend Saturday at an outdoor market. Olga wanted a plant for her room, and Leila wanted to try the food stalls. They agreed to meet outside the library at eleven.

At ten thirty, heavy rain started. Leila sent a message: "Should we stay at home?"

Olga was already on the bus. "I'm nearly there. Let's meet and decide."

When Leila arrived, Olga was standing under the library roof. Her shoes were wet, but she was laughing.

"The market can wait," she said. "What's inside here?"

They usually borrowed books from the library and left quickly. That morning, a sign near the door said: "Free board games upstairs. Everyone welcome."

Neither friend knew the rules of the game on the nearest table. A woman called Grace explained them and asked them to join. At first, Olga made several mistakes. Soon, everyone was helping each other and laughing.

After the game, they sat in the little library café. Leila drew a funny picture of Olga's wet shoes on a paper napkin.

"We didn't buy anything," Olga said.

"And we didn't spend the afternoon looking at our phones," Leila replied.

The rain stopped at three. On the way home, they passed a flower shop. Olga bought a small plant there. It cost less than the bus ticket, and she gave it a name: Saturday.
